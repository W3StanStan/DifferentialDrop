**Differential Drop**

Play the game here:
*[differential-drop.vercel.app](differential-drop.vercel.app)*

Just a heads up, most people have had a better time with the resolution playing this game fullscreened!

This project is a short math game I developed as a submission for my AP Calculus BC final junior year of high school.

Given free rein to employ a calculus concept in an engaging presentation, I decided combining the knowledge of calculus I had picked up with my passion for game development would be the way to go. In the end, I decided to develop a game where the player finds constants of integration to position graphs that guide physical objects.

Other than the game engine (Unity) and the font I used (Raster Forge by GGBotNet), everything in this project was made by me - coding, music, graphics, sound effects and all:
Coded with Unity C#
Music made using Soundtrap
Graphics made with Piskel, animations made with linear interpolation, particle effects, and built-in animation system
Sound effects made with JSFXR

Overall, while it may have felt like I'd bitten off more than I could chew at times during the development process, I learned firsthand the humbling amount of work that goes into delivering a presentable product - in this case, a nifty little game about indefinite integration.
